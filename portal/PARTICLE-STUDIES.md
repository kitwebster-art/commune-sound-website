# Commune Sound interactive wordmark particle studies

These five studies are draft-only and do not alter the default homepage unless
their `particle-study` query is present.

1. [Magnetic Dust](https://communesound.com.au/?particle-study=magnetic-dust)
2. [Neon Swarm](https://communesound.com.au/?particle-study=neon-swarm)
3. [Prismatic Shatter](https://communesound.com.au/?particle-study=prismatic-shatter)
4. [Sonic Wave](https://communesound.com.au/?particle-study=sonic-wave)
5. [Vortex Portal](https://communesound.com.au/?particle-study=vortex-portal)

All studies sample the currently selected transparent wordmark. Hovering breaks
the image into particles around the pointer, clicking triggers a larger event,
and the letters reassemble when interaction ends. Each mode dispatches a named
music response to the generative soundscape.

## Inspection controls

- `particle-seed=413` sets the deterministic particle selection.
- `particle-quality=low|high` selects the fixed particle budget.
- `particle-debug=final|source|field|particles|no-post` isolates the source,
  interaction field, particle pool or unblended baseline.
- `wordmark=liquid-chrome` fixes the banner source for like-for-like comparison.

The default visual contract is a recognisable source silhouette at rest, local
breakup on hover, a stronger click envelope, full reassembly, mobile touch
support and no effect for reduced-motion users.
